**Subject:** educ_uoe_mobs02 – Belgium, students from France, 2024: break in series?

Hello,

In the dataset educ_uoe_mobs02 (degree-mobile students by country of origin), the number of tertiary students (ISCED 5-8) enrolled in Belgium whose country of origin is France falls from 19,104 in 2023 to 14,019 in 2024, a decrease of about 27 %, whereas it had stayed between 18,000 and 19,800 since 2020. Neither 2023 nor 2024 carries a flag; the 2020 value is flagged "b" (break in time series) and "d" (definition differs).

Could you tell us whether the 2024 value reflects a change in how Belgium determines the country of origin (prior education vs. citizenship), a change in coverage (for instance one of the communities or some institutions), or an actual fall in enrolments? If a break is confirmed, will a flag be added?

Data retrieved through the API on 10 October 2026 (dataset last updated 6 October 2026), query: `educ_uoe_mobs02?partner=FR&isced11=ED5-8&sex=T&unit=NR&sinceTimePeriod=2013`.
